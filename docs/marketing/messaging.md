# DirectWork messaging

Pre-launch messaging for DirectWork. Everything here has to be true of the
product as built (or, for the Deal Sheet and pays-on-time record, as specified
in `docs/specs/deal-sheet.md` and shipped before launch). Read the claims
checklist at the end before writing anything new.

Placeholders: `[CITY]` (first launch city, likely Leeds), `[PRICE]` (Pro price;
£29/month is still a starting guess in `docs/MONETIZATION.md`), `[LAUNCH DATE]`,
`[URL]`, `[EMAIL]`.

## Positioning

**Line:** Know the terms. Know they pay.

**Statement:** DirectWork is a curated UK marketplace for paid work and
co-founder opportunities at early-stage ventures and small businesses. A person
reviews every listing before it goes live. Both sides agree the terms in writing
before work starts, and owners carry a record of how they reply and how they
pay, reported by the professionals who worked with them.

**What it is not:** a social network. No feed, no likes, no followers, no
posting for attention. You come to find work or find people, then you leave and
do the work.

**The problem, in three facts:**

- Freelancers spend about 20 days a year chasing payments, and 43% have done
  work they were never paid for. (IPSE)
- Only 54% of invoices are paid on time. (FreeAgent)
- Applying for work often means hearing nothing back.

Most of that starts with work agreed informally: a call, a few messages, a
"let's just get going". DirectWork doesn't handle money. It does two things
instead: it makes the terms explicit before work starts, and it makes payment
behaviour visible afterwards.

**How the tagline holds up.** "Know the terms" is the Deal Sheet: scope, pay,
payment terms, milestones and IP, confirmed by both sides. "Know they pay" is the
pays-on-time record: a count of payments reported by professionals, shown once
an owner has 3 or more undisputed reports. The tagline must always sit near an
explanation of what the record is. It is a record of past reports, not a promise
about the future.

## Audiences

### 1. Aspiring freelancers and graduates

People starting out: recent graduates, career changers, people taking their
first freelance or side work.

**What they worry about:** not being taken seriously without a track record;
being asked to work for "exposure"; scams and paid "tests"; not knowing what to
put in writing; not getting paid.

**Value proposition:** Start your track record on work where the terms are
written down and you never pay to apply.

**Proof points:**

- Professionals never pay. No application credits, no fees, no paid boosts.
- Apply with a short note and up to 3 work samples. No CV wall, no cover-letter
  essay.
- A person reviews every listing before it goes live. Partner listings must
  state what already exists, the weekly hours, the first milestone, the
  proposed terms and what the owner is contributing.
- Your contact details stay private until you and the owner both accept.
- When you're accepted, you see a "Before you start" card: DirectWork never
  asks for money, never pay to get work, agree pay, scope and IP in writing,
  check companies on Companies House.
- The Deal Sheet gives you a simple written record of scope, pay, payment terms,
  milestones and who owns the work. You don't need to know how to draft a
  contract to use it.
- Each finished piece of work becomes part of a track record confirmed by both
  sides, with references.

### 2. Working professionals

Experienced freelancers, contractors and specialists who already have clients
and limited time.

**What they worry about:** time wasted on vague briefs and tyre-kickers; equity
offers with nothing behind them; chasing invoices; owners who go quiet.

**Value proposition:** See how an owner replies and pays before you spend time
on them, and agree the terms before you start.

**Proof points:**

- Owners show a reply record: how many applicants they replied to in the last
  180 days and how quickly they usually reply.
- Owners show a pays-on-time record, e.g. "Paid on time on 4 of 5 reported
  payments", once 3 or more undisputed reports exist.
- Every listing is reviewed by a person before it goes live. Partner listings
  must set out what's built, the time commitment, the first milestone and the
  proposed equity or terms.
- Owners can ask up to 3 questions, so you answer what matters instead of
  rewriting a cover letter.
- The Deal Sheet covers scope, pay basis, payment terms (default 14 days),
  milestones, IP and, for partner deals, equity, vesting, cliff and decision
  rights. Both sides confirm the same version. Any edit needs confirming again.
- For business-to-business deals, a prefilled late-payment letter citing the
  Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, with statutory interest
  and fixed compensation worked out. It is a template, not legal advice.
- Either side can save a dated PDF of the agreed Deal Sheet.

### 3. Owners and founders

Early-stage ventures, small businesses, studios and founders looking for paid
help or a co-founder or partner.

**What they worry about:** hundreds of low-effort applications; not being able
to tell who's serious; good people not trusting an unknown business; the cost of
job boards.

**Value proposition:** Reach professionals who take you seriously, because your
listing has been reviewed and your record shows you reply and pay.

**Proof points:**

- Every listing is reviewed by a person, which keeps the marketplace worth a
  professional's time. That's the reason they'll read yours.
- Applications are short and comparable: a note, up to 3 work samples and
  answers to up to 3 questions you set.
- A reply record and a pays-on-time record you build by doing what you said.
  Good owners get a visible advantage they can't otherwise show.
- The Deal Sheet gets scope and payment terms agreed early, which cuts down
  arguments later.
- Contact details are shared only when you both accept, so your inbox isn't
  public.
- Free to post one live listing. Pro (`[PRICE]`) gives up to 10 live listings and
  Featured placement at the top of Discover. Featured is labelled and never
  skips review.

## Objection handling

| Objection                                               | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Isn't this just another job board?"                    | Job boards are built for volume. DirectWork reviews every listing by hand, keeps applications short, and shows how owners reply and pay. There's no feed and nothing to scroll for its own sake.                                                                                                |
| "Is this LinkedIn?"                                     | No. No posts, no likes, no followers. Profiles exist to apply for and offer work, and the track record is confirmed by both sides of real work.                                                                                                                                                 |
| "Do you check the people on it?"                        | A person reviews every listing before it goes live. We don't run identity checks on users, and we say so. When you're accepted, the app shows a safety card and you can check companies on Companies House. You can report or block anyone.                                                     |
| "Will I definitely get paid?"                           | No platform can promise that, and DirectWork never handles money. What it does: the terms are written down and confirmed by both of you, owners' payment records are visible, and for business-to-business deals there's a late-payment letter template.                                        |
| "Is the Deal Sheet a contract?"                         | It's a written record of terms you both confirmed. DirectWork doesn't draft it and isn't a party to it. For anything significant, especially equity, get your own legal advice.                                                                                                                 |
| "What stops a professional posting a false 'not paid'?" | Reports can only be made on a Deal Sheet both sides confirmed, after the due date. Owners can dispute any report within 14 days, and disputed reports don't count until an admin resolves them. New accounts can't create counting reports. Reporting falsely is a serious breach of our terms. |
| "What stops an owner gaming their own record?"          | Reports come from professionals, one per milestone, only on agreed deals. We don't accept paid or incentivised reports and we don't hide genuine negative ones.                                                                                                                                 |
| "Why do professionals not pay?"                         | Charging people to find work is restricted in the UK, and professionals are the people who make the marketplace worth joining. Owners pay for reach, if they want more.                                                                                                                         |
| "I'm new. Will anyone accept me without a record?"      | Applications are a short note and up to 3 samples, so the work speaks first. Everyone's record starts at zero.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| "Why only [CITY]?"                                      | Curation needs a person reviewing listings and getting to know the local market. We'd rather do one city properly than the whole country badly.                                                                                                                                                 |
| "Why is the pay record hidden for some owners?"         | It only appears once there are 3 or more undisputed reports, so one report can't define anyone. New owners show "New to hiring here" instead.                                                                                                                                                   |
| "Is there an app?"                                      | One app for web, iPhone and Android. Say only what's actually available on the day; see the checklist.                                                                                                                                                                                          |

## Claims checklist

Use this before publishing anything. If a line isn't covered here, ask before
using it.

### We may say

| Claim                                                                     | Why it's allowed                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "A person reviews every listing before it goes live."                     | True of the product. Listings go through review before publishing.                                                                 |
| "Professionals never pay." / "Free for professionals."                    | Product rule in `docs/MONETIZATION.md`, also required by UK rules on charging work-seekers.                                        |
| "Apply with a short note and up to 3 work samples."                       | Matches the application flow.                                                                                                      |
| "Owners can ask up to 3 questions."                                       | Matches the listing form.                                                                                                          |
| "Contact details are shared only when you both accept."                   | Contact reveal happens on mutual acceptance.                                                                                       |
| "A track record confirmed by both sides."                                 | Outcomes require both parties to confirm.                                                                                          |
| "Paid on time on X of Y reported payments." / "reported by professionals" | Exact factual wording from the spec. Always use "reported".                                                                        |
| "Shown once there are 3 or more undisputed reports."                      | Spec rule. Use it whenever we explain the record.                                                                                  |
| "Owners can dispute a report."                                            | Spec rule, and it's what makes the record fair. Mention it to owners.                                                              |
| "Owners show a reply record."                                             | Built: replies in the last 180 days and typical reply time.                                                                        |
| "A Deal Sheet both sides confirm."                                        | Spec: both must confirm the same version; edits reset confirmations.                                                               |
| "A late-payment letter template for business-to-business deals."          | Spec. Always say "template", "business-to-business", and "not legal advice" when there's room.                                     |
| "No feed, no likes, no followers."                                        | Product boundary in `CLAUDE.md`.                                                                                                   |
| The IPSE and FreeAgent figures, with the source named.                    | Published research. Before each use, check the figure, the population it covers and the year against the source page, and link it. |
| "Launching in [CITY]."                                                    | Once the city is decided.                                                                                                          |

### We may not say

| Claim                                                                                          | Why not                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Verified" (users, owners, payers, records, references)                                        | We don't verify identity, and the pay record is user reports. "Verified" would mislead under the CAP Code and the DMCC Act. Use "confirmed by both sides" or "reported by professionals". |
| "Guaranteed payment", "get paid on time", "never chase an invoice again", "protected payments" | DirectWork never handles money and can't guarantee anything about payment.                                                                                                                |
| "Can't be faked", "fake-proof", "tamper-proof"                                                 | We have controls (account age, agreed deals only, disputes) but no system is impossible to game. Overclaiming accuracy of reviews is a DMCC Act risk.                                     |
| "Know they'll pay" or "trusted payers"                                                         | The record is past reports, not a prediction. The tagline "Know they pay" is fine only alongside an explanation of the record.                                                            |
| "Bad payer", "unreliable", "blacklist", "rated" or any score                                   | The spec forbids labels and scores. They create defamation risk and look like a credit reference.                                                                                         |
| "Credit check", "payment history check", "background check"                                    | Credit references are FCA-regulated; we are not providing one.                                                                                                                            |
| "Legally binding contract", "lawyer-approved", "watertight"                                    | DirectWork doesn't draft the Deal Sheet or give legal advice, and the solicitor review isn't finished.                                                                                    |
| "Vetted professionals", "top talent", "the best freelancers"                                   | We review listings, not people. Superlatives need evidence we don't have.                                                                                                                 |
| "Safe", "scam-free", "100% secure"                                                             | We reduce risk (review, safety card, reporting, blocking); we can't remove it.                                                                                                            |
| "Never get ghosted" / "owners always reply"                                                    | The reply record shows behaviour; it doesn't force a reply.                                                                                                                               |
| "Thousands of listings", "join 5,000 professionals" or any number                              | We have none yet. Only publish numbers we can show.                                                                                                                                       |
| Testimonials, quotes or case studies we haven't collected                                      | Fake or invented reviews are banned under the DMCC Act. Use real quotes with written permission, or none.                                                                                 |
| "The UK's first..." or "only..."                                                               | Unverifiable.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| "Download on the App Store / Google Play"                                                      | Only once the store listings are live.                                                                                                                                                    |
| A specific Pro price                                                                           | `[PRICE]` until the commit-to-pay test sets it.                                                                                                                                           |
| Describing the Deal Sheet or pay record as live before they ship                               | They're specified, not built. Until then say "at launch" or "coming before launch", and label any mock-up "Illustration".                                                                 |
| "Free forever" for owners                                                                      | Owner pricing may change. Only the professionals rule is fixed.                                                                                                                           |
| "AI-matched" or "smart matching"                                                               | There's no matching engine, and there won't be one.                                                                                                                                       |

### Tone

Plain, confident British English. Short sentences. Say what happens, not how
it'll make someone feel. Avoid: revolutionary, game-changing, seamless,
empower, unlock, supercharge, effortless, next-level, ecosystem, community (as
a selling point), journey. No emoji in headings or as bullets.
