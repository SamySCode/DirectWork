# DirectWork video scripts

Five vertical shorts (9:16, 15–45 seconds) and one 90-second explainer. All
footage uses real app screens recorded from a seeded test environment with
fictional data. No real users' names, faces or details.

Rules for every video:

- Burn in captions; most people watch muted.
- On-screen text follows `messaging.md`. Never show "verified", "guaranteed" or
  a score.
- The Deal Sheet, payment reporting and the pays-on-time record aren't built
  yet. Capture them only once they ship. Until then, either hold those videos or
  show a design mock-up labelled "Illustration – arriving at launch" on screen
  for the whole shot.
- Any owner record shown must be realistic for the rules: at least 3 reports
  ("Paid on time on 4 of 5 reported payments"), never a perfect-looking
  "100%" headline.
- End card on all: DirectWork logo · "Launching in [CITY]" · [URL].
