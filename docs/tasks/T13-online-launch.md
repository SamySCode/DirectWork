# T13: Online-only launch plan

**Model:** Sonnet. **Size:** one session. Web research needed.

## Goal

The owner will not do in-person events or meetings. Write
`docs/marketing/online-launch.md`: a launch plan for 4 January to
4 April 2027 that uses only online channels and fits a solo founder
with a full-time job (about 5 to 8 hours a week).

## Read first

`docs/ROADMAP.md`, `docs/marketing/messaging.md` (the claims checklist
is binding), `docs/marketing/competitors.md` sections 4 and 6, and the
partner list in `docs/marketing/city-launch.md`.

## Write

1. **Leeds first online, or UK-wide in one niche?** Compare the two for
   an online-only launch and recommend one. Density matters for a
   two-sided marketplace; a city is one way to get it and a narrow niche
   is another.
2. **Channels, ranked by expected sign-ups per hour of effort**, each
   with what to post, how often, the rules of that community (many ban
   self-promotion: check and cite), and cost. Cover at least: LinkedIn
   (personal posts, not ads), relevant UK subreddits, Indie Hackers,
   founder and freelancer Slack and Discord communities with UK or
   Yorkshire members, university enterprise and careers teams reached
   by email, coworking and accelerator newsletters, Product Hunt, and
   UK freelancer communities such as IPSE's. Name real communities and
   check they exist and are active.
3. **Supply first.** A marketplace with no listings loses the
   professionals it attracts. Plan how to get the first 15 good
   listings online before inviting professionals (direct messages to
   founders who are publicly hiring, with a message template).
4. **Messages to partners by email only**: templates for a university
   enterprise team, a coworking newsletter editor and an accelerator.
   These are one-to-one business emails from the owner's own mailbox;
   say what UK marketing rules (PECR) allow for that.
5. **Owner interviews by video call**: how to recruit ten, and the
   question list.
6. **A week-by-week calendar** for the 13 weeks, with hours per week.
7. **Re-estimated numbers** for day 30, 60 and 90 (professional
   sign-ups, approved listings, contact exchanges, confirmed outcomes)
   for an online-only launch, with the reasoning. The existing ranges
   in `docs/ROADMAP.md` assumed weekly in-person outreach.
8. **What not to do**: paid ads before day 60, buying lists, scraping
   contact details, mass direct messages, posting in communities that
   forbid promotion.

## Don't

Edit only `docs/marketing/online-launch.md`. Don't invent community
names, member counts or prices: cite a URL or mark it unverified.

## Done when

The file exists, every named community has a link, and
`npx prettier --check docs/marketing/online-launch.md` passes.
